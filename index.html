The Formula

Seven Botanicals.
One Transformative Result.

Every ingredient is selected for a singular purpose. Nothing decorative. Nothing superfluous. Each element earns its place through thousands of years of Ayurvedic wisdom and modern clinical validation.

01
Cocos Nucifera
Coconut · Cold-Pressed Base · Kerala, India

The sacred foundation of every great Indian hair ritual. Cold-pressed from mature coconuts grown along the Kerala coast, this unrefined base oil penetrates the hair shaft at a molecular level — a feat no synthetic conditioning agent can replicate. Its lauric acid profile binds to hair protein, actively reducing breakage from within.

02
Allium Cepa
Onion · Red Variety Extract · North India

The most clinically validated ingredient in modern Ayurvedic hair science. Rich in sulphur — the essential building block of keratin — onion extract has been shown in peer-reviewed studies to dramatically increase hair density and reduce shedding. Where science and tradition finally speak the same language.

03
Eclipta Alba
Bhringraj · The King of Herbs · Ayurvedic Grade

Called Kesharaj — "ruler of the hair" — in ancient Ayurvedic texts, Bhringraj has been used for millennia to stimulate scalp circulation and awaken dormant follicles. Its wedelolactone compounds work at the cellular level, extending each hair's active growth phase and commanding the scalp back into productivity.

04
Emblica Officinalis
Amla · Indian Gooseberry · Himalayan Foothills

One of the most vitamin C-dense plants on earth, amla has anchored Indian hair care for over 3,000 years. It fortifies the hair from root to tip, seals the cuticle against environmental damage, and imparts a natural luminosity that no synthetic shine serum can simulate. The reason Indian hair has been envied across civilisations.

05
Rosmarinus Officinalis
Rosemary · Steam-Distilled · Ceremonial Grade

Now the subject of landmark clinical research, rosemary oil has been shown to match prescription minoxidil in stimulating hair regrowth — without the side effects. Its ursolic acid increases microcirculation in the scalp, delivering nutrients directly to the follicle. Ancient Mediterranean wisdom, finally validated by modern science.

06
Bacopa Monnieri
Brahmi · Sacred Herb · Gangetic Plains

Named after Brahma, the Hindu creator deity, Brahmi has been revered in Ayurvedic medicine for over 3,000 years as the herb of intelligence and renewal. Applied to the scalp, its triterpenoid saponins strengthen the hair root, soothe chronic inflammation, and create the calm, nourished scalp environment in which truly beautiful hair is possible.

07
Hibiscus Rosa-Sinensis
Hibiscus · Flower & Leaf · South India

The flower that Indian women have woven into their hair for generations — not merely for beauty, but for purpose. Hibiscus is rich in amino acids that directly nourish the hair follicle, natural mucilage that seals moisture into every strand, and flavonoids that inhibit the enzyme responsible for hair loss. Beauty and biology in perfect accord.
